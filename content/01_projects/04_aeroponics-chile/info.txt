TITLE: Frieden Electric (Startup Chile)
DATES: 2017 - 2018
WHERE: Santiago, Chile
MY ROLE: Co-founder
ONE-LINE SUMMARY: Took the aeroponics company to Santiago on a Startup Chile grant, built two more systems, grew microgreens and gave free samples to local shops.
THE PROBLEM: Grow food anywhere, without soil, for the people who need it most.
WHAT I BUILT / DID:
- Won a $15,000 grant from the Chilean government through Startup Chile, plus a one-year residency visa and co-working space in Santiago
- Built two more aeroponic systems in Chile
- Grew microgreens and gave free samples to local shops to find customers
- Spoke to college students about the company
RESULTS: $15,000 grant · Startup Chile · PEC incubated · Rs 10 lakh award
WHAT WENT WRONG / WHAT I LEARNED:
We didn't speak the language and knew too little about sales to turn free samples into real customers, so we came back.
The bigger lesson came later. I'd started this to fix food insecurity in India, thinking the problem was getting food to remote places. Over time I realised the food is usually available; a large part of the population just can't afford it. India's food insecurity is more economic than logistical. If someone can't afford a kilo of wheat or rice, they certainly can't buy and run an aeroponic system. That's why it ended.
