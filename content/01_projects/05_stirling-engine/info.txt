TITLE: Stirling Engine
DATES: 2015
WHERE: PEC University of Technology, Chandigarh. Presented in Paris, France.
MY ROLE: Researcher & co-author, with my friend K. S. Ghumman
ONE-LINE SUMMARY: We built a Stirling engine that makes electricity from a cooking fire, then presented it at an international conference in Paris.
THE PROBLEM: Many rural homes have no reliable electricity, but they cook over a fire every day. All that heat goes to waste.
WHAT I BUILT / DID:
- My friend K. S. Ghumman wanted to write a research paper. We decided to build a machine that generates electricity from cooking fire, and we did.
- Built a thermodynamic model of the engine that accounts for convection heat transfer to the working fluid
- Wrote two papers and presented both at the International Congress on Energy and Environment Engineering and Management, Paris, 22-24 July 2015
TOOLS / TECH / SKILLS USED: Thermodynamics, heat transfer, mechanical design, machining, research writing
RESULTS: Oral presentation · Poster presentation · Paris 2015
PAPERS:
- Thermodynamic Model of Stirling Engine Considering Convection Heat Transfer to Working Fluid (oral presentation, ref. RE107)
- Development of a Low Cost Stirling Cycle Based Engine to Produce Power in Remote Areas (poster presentation, ref. RE108)
