TITLE: What Does a Smart Plug Leak?
STATUS: In progress
DATES: 2026
MY ROLE: Investigator (and the person who built the plug)
ONE-LINE SUMMARY: Capturing the network traffic of my own Happy Plug with Wireshark to see what a cheap IoT device gives away.

WHAT I BUILT / DID:
- Put the plug on an isolated network and captured its traffic with Wireshark
- Looked at the WiFi provisioning, the MQTT messages and anything sent in plain text
- Compared it with what a modern, properly secured device should do
