About Me

Engineer · Builder · Cybersecurity

I'm a mechanical engineer turned software developer turned cybersecurity student. I like building real things: drones, smart plugs, aeroponic farms, websites and apps. I started a company in India, took it to Startup Chile, came to Canada and worked my way up from labourer to Construction Safety Officer, while freelancing as a developer on the side. Drone

A series of home-built aircraft I designed during my Mechanical Engineering degree. None of them flew, and I learned more from them than from anything that did.

- Built multiple versions over the course of my B.Tech, each one fixing what the last got wrong - Cut the foam aerofoil wings with a foam cutter I built at home - Spent a long time machining a twin swashplate mechanism out of aluminum - Learned about balsa wood, electric motors, ESCs, and the difference between centre of weight and centre of pressure

This version didn't fly. The modified versions after it didn't fly either.

Happy Plug

A WiFi smart plug that let you switch your room's lights and fan from your phone, built and sold door to door before smart plugs were common in India.

- Started with an IR remote to switch room lights and fans, then moved to an Android app talking to a WiFi microcontroller driving relays - Built the first working units and took them door to door to real homes - Redesigned the enclosure several times (thin, thick, square and triangle versions) to make it look like a product, then took it shop to shop - Designed stickers, packaging and three final models, and got it market ready - Pitched to shops and then a distributor, who asked for more testing before taking it on

Market-ready product · Three models · Pitched to a distributor

Stirling Engine

We built a Stirling engine that makes electricity from a cooking fire, then presented it at an international conference in Paris.

- My friend K. S. Ghumman wanted to write a research paper. We decided to build a machine that generates electricity from cooking fire, and we did. - Built a thermodynamic model of the engine that accounts for convection heat transfer to the working fluid - Wrote two papers and presented both at the International Congress on Energy and Environment Engineering and Management, Paris, 22-24 July 2015

Oral presentation · Poster presentation · Paris 2015
